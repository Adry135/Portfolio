Marketing & Campaigns

Marketing couldn't say which campaign actually drove pipeline.

Problem: email blasts, social posts, and paid ads were tracked in isolation, with no shared tagging convention — so nobody could tell which channel turned a click into a paying customer.

campaign-performance.dashboard
Open rate % by campaign
Sessions by channel, last 6 months
Conversion funnel: click → lead → opportunity → won
ROI % by campaign
Challenge
Built a consistent UTM and click-ID tagging convention so every session could be traced from ad click to closed deal in the CRM.
Result
Marketing could finally rank campaigns by pipeline contribution, not just clicks — budget reallocated toward what actually converted.
